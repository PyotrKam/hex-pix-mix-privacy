# Privacy Policy — Bearer of the Scroll

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Hex Pix Mix develops Bearer of the Scroll (Android package:
com.KamyakGames.BearerOfTheScroll).

## Data handled by the game

The current version does not collect or transmit personal data to
Hex Pix Mix. It does not require an account and does not use active
advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting services.

Game progress, completed and unlocked levels, and remaining lives
are stored locally on your device to provide save and resume
functionality. Hex Pix Mix does not receive these save files.

## Sharing

We do not sell or share personal data collected through the game.
Google Play and your device’s operating system may process
information independently under their own privacy policies.

## Storage and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until it is cleared or
removed. You can delete it through Android Settings by opening
the app’s storage settings and selecting Clear storage or Clear
data. This resets your progress. Device backup and restoration
may be controlled separately by your operating system settings.

## Contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information
you include. We use this information to respond to your request.
Messages are processed through our email provider, Gmail, and
retained only as needed to handle your request or meet applicable
legal obligations. You can request deletion by contacting us.

Access to support correspondence is restricted to those handling
your request. Please do not send passwords or other sensitive
information.

## Intended audience

The game is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not
knowingly collect personal information from children through
the game.

## Changes

We will update this policy if the game’s data practices change,
including before introducing advertising or analytics that
collect data.
